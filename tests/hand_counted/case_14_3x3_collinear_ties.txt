# p and q are two parallel horizontal lines, offset by 1 in y. Every
# diagonal distance dist(p[i],q[i]) equals 1, and every adjacent
# cross-distance dist(p[i],q[i+1])/dist(p[i+1],q[i]) equals sqrt(2), by
# symmetry. This creates repeated exact ties throughout the grid, not just
# at one cell, exercising the A > B > C tie-break rule over and over.

algorithm BBMSInter
